1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the business requirements for a mobile and web-based cricket management application. The app will streamline team management, match scoring, player participation tracking, payments, invoicing, and data-driven performance analysis. The aim is to simplify cricket operations for amateur and corporate teams, enhance team coordination, ensure financial transparency, and offer strategic insights to improve gameplay.

⸻

2. Business Objectives
	•	Provide an all-in-one platform for managing cricket teams and matches.
	•	Automate and digitize the scoring process.
	•	Collect and store data for individual and team performance tracking.
	•	Enable seamless communication of player availability to team managers.
	•	Ensure easy and transparent financial transactions for players and teams.
	•	Provide match insights and analytics to improve team strategies and outcomes.

⸻

3. Scope

In Scope
	•	Match scoring with real-time and post-match data collection.
	•	Player availability tracking by team managers/admins.
	•	Payment tracking for players based on match participation.
	•	Invoice generation and tracking by corporate organizers.
	•	In-app payment functionality.
	•	Analytics and recommendations based on performance data.

Out of Scope
	•	International or professional league integration.
	•	Video streaming or broadcasting of matches.

⸻

4. Functional Requirements

4.1 Match Scoring Module
	•	Support for all cricket formats (T20, ODI, Test).
	•	Real-time score updating and display.
	•	Track player stats: runs, wickets, strike rate, economy, etc.
	•	Store match data for historical analysis.
	•	Exportable match summaries and statistics.

4.2 Player Availability Module
	•	Players update their availability per match.
	•	Admins/managers view availability status in dashboard.
	•	Notifications/reminders for players to update availability.
	•	Lock roster for upcoming matches by manager.

4.3 Payment Module for Players
	•	Track player participation per match.
	•	Generate individual payment requests post-match.
	•	Support in-app payment (UPI/Credit/Debit/Net Banking).
	•	Payment history and pending dues section for players.

4.4 Invoice Management by Organizers
	•	Corporates create and send invoices for matches conducted.
	•	Managers receive and review invoices in-app.
	•	Managers make payments on behalf of the team via app.
	•	Invoice history and receipts available in dashboard.

4.5 Performance Analytics Module
	•	Generate insights like:
	•	Win/loss trends based on player performance.
	•	Key players and match-winners.
	•	Areas of weakness (e.g., middle-over collapses).
	•	Suggest strategy improvements.
	•	Personalized player performance dashboards.

⸻

5. Non-Functional Requirements
	•	Mobile and web compatibility.
	•	Secure login and role-based access (Admin, Player, Organizer).
	•	Data encryption and secure payment gateway integration.
	•	Responsive and intuitive user interface.
	•	Scalable architecture to support multiple teams and tournaments.

⸻

6. User Roles and Permissions

Role	Permissions
Player	View schedule, update availability, view/pay match fees, view personal stats
Admin/Manager	Manage team, view player availability, manage roster, view/pay invoices, access team analytics
Organizer	Schedule matches, send invoices, view team rosters, track payments



⸻

7. Assumptions and Constraints
	•	Users have access to smartphones or computers.
	•	Internet connection is required for real-time updates.
